## 1. Brand Research

### Brand Overview
Oliver Co London is a B Corp-certified premium vegan accessories brand based in London. Founded to prove that sustainability and luxury are not mutually exclusive, the brand makes wallets, card holders, and accessories from pioneering bio-based materials — without animal products, and with full supply chain transparency.

**Positioning:** Sustainable luxury. The premium alternative to both fast-fashion accessories and traditional leather goods.

### Brand Voice & Tone
- Confident but not preachy about sustainability
- Intelligent and considered — speaks to conscious consumers, not activists
- Warm London craft aesthetic — quality-first, values-close
- Copy leans aspirational without being unattainable

**Taglines / Brand Phrases Found:**
- "Style, sustainability, and durability can exist together in one essential design."
- "Sustainably considered throughout."
- "Built to last."
- "Progressive accessories with low environmental impact."

### Hero Products & Pricing

| Compact Wallet | £65 |

**USPs:**
- Made from Apple Leather (50% apple waste from Bolzano, Italy fruit juice industry) or WASTEA® (tea leaf bio-material)

- 9 pockets in the Premium Compact Wallet
- Recycled lining — GRS certified + OEKO-TEX certified (no toxic materials)

### Awards & Press Validation
- **IndyBest (The Independent):** Best Wallet

## 2. Audience & Competitor Research

### Target Customer Profile

**Primary Segment — The Values-Led Professional**
- Age: 28–42
- Location: London, major UK cities (Manchester, Edinburgh, Bristol)
- Income: £40k+
- Gender: Male and female skew (males slightly dominant in wallet category)
- Psychographics:
  - Conscious consumer but not a militant vegan — sustainability is one value among many
  - Design-literate; notices quality materials and craftsmanship
  - Wears Allbirds, drinks oat milk, uses a Bellroy bag, reads the Guardian
  - Carries a worn-out old wallet and has been meaning to upgrade for months
  - Influenced by peer carry choices; wallet is a small but daily status signal

**Secondary Segment — The Conscious Gift Buyer**
- Age: 30–55
- Purchasing for a partner, spouse, or friend
- Searches: "sustainable gift for him", "vegan leather wallet UK", "best wallet gift"
- High intent, higher AOV (often goes Premium + personalisation)

### Competitor Ad Analysis

| Brand | Winning Ad Angles | Hook Type |
|---|---|---|
| Bellroy | Slim = smartness (the over-stuffed wallet problem) | Problem/solution, product demo |
| Troubadour | Material craftsmanship close-up | Texture/aspiration, no dialogue |
| Mismo | Lifestyle aspiration, editorial | Silent lifestyle, text overlay |
| Paperthinks | Sustainability story, material origin | Brand story, UGC feel |

**What converts in this category (Meta 2025):**
1. Texture close-ups in the first 2 seconds — tactile content stops the scroll
2. The "old wallet vs. new wallet" problem frame works across all funnel stages
3. Award/press badges dramatically lift ROAS for cold traffic (social proof collapses objections)
4. Price revelation at the end of copy outperforms leading with price
5. UGC-style authenticity outperforms polished studio — but for premium brands, cinematic editorial beats both

**Common Objections:**
- "Is vegan leather as durable as real leather?" → Counter: 2-year guarantee, apple leather durability claims
- "Is £89 worth it?" → Counter: cost-per-use framing, longevity, personalisation as gift justification
- "I've never heard of this brand" → Counter: press badges, B Corp, award logos

## 4. Video Strategy — 6-Second Hook

**Angle Name:** Texture Reveal
**Funnel Stage:** Awareness / Scroll-stop (cold traffic)
**Format:** 9:16 (Reels + Stories), 15 seconds (Hyper Motion edit)
**Model Used:** Marketing Studio — Hyper Motion preset

**Shot Sequence:**
1. **0–2s:** Extreme close-up of apple leather grain — warm light catching the texture. No context. Pure tactile curiosity.
2. **2–5s:** Camera pulls back slowly. A hand holds the slim wallet. A London street (Shoreditch brick) comes into focus behind. Urban, aspirational, still.
3. **5–6s:** Hold on wallet + blurred London backdrop. Simple tagline: **"Made from apples. Built for life."**

**Why it works:**
- Texture content performs 40% better for premium accessories in the 3-second view rate metric
- No voice-over required — works with sound off (85% of Meta feed plays muted)
- The apple origin creates a genuine "wait, what?" scroll-stopper moment
- Ends on the product alone — no human face competes for attention in the close frame

## 6. Campaign Setup Recommendations

### Campaign Structure
```
Campaign: Oliver Co — Conversion (Purchase)
├── Ad Set 1: Cold — Interest targeting (sustainability, vegan lifestyle, premium accessories)
│   ├── Ad 1A: Image — Problem/Solution (Desktop Feed)
│   └── Ad 1B: Video — Texture Reveal Hook (Reels/Stories)
├── Ad Set 2: Cold — Lookalike (1% UK, based on purchaser list)
│   ├── Ad 2A: Image — Aspiration
│   └── Ad 2B: Video — Texture Reveal Hook
└── Ad Set 3: Warm — Retargeting (website visitors, video viewers 50%+)
    ├── Ad 3A: Image — Social Proof
    └── Ad 3B: Image — Product Feature
```

### Targeting Recommendations
- **Cold interests:** Sustainable fashion, vegan lifestyle, Bellroy, premium accessories, The Guardian readers, B Corp, ethical shopping
- **Cold lookalike:** 1% UK LAL from customer email list
- **Warm retargeting:** Website visitors (30 days), video viewers (50%+ of hook video), Instagram engagers (60 days)
- **Exclude:** Recent purchasers (30 days)

### Budget Split (recommended starter)
- Cold Video Hook: 40% of budget
- Cold Image — Problem/Solution: 25%
- Cold Image — Aspiration: 15%
- Retargeting (Social Proof + Feature): 20%
