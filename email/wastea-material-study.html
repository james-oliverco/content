— WASTEA®  ·  LOT 01 / 26

On material

"Made from what
was left."

WASTEA® was co-developed exclusively for Oliver Co. from leftover leaves of the tea-making industry in Turkey — naturally dried alongside the raw tea production process, transformed into a 65% bio-content material through bio-polyurethane bonding onto recycled polyester canvas.

No synthetic drying. No additional energy. The leaves were always going to dry — we gave them somewhere to go.
